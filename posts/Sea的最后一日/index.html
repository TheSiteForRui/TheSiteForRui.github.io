Dear colleagues and friends,

As some of you may know, today marks my last day at Sea. Before I go, I wanted to take a moment to thank each of you.

Since joining the company in 2019 after graduation, this place has become a second home to me—a space filled with friendship, growth, and countless moments of laughter. Reflecting on my time here, I realize that more than half of my 20s have been intertwined with Sea, and these years will forever hold a special place in my heart.

It has been an absolute privilege to work alongside such talented, kind, and humble individuals. Stepping into new roles and responsibilities was both challenging and exhilarating, and I could not have done it without your support and encouragement. The lessons I’ve learned and the relationships I’ve built here have profoundly shaped who I am today.
